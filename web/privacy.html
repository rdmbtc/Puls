Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026
Puls is a testnet product. It is an experimental hackathon prototype. We collect the minimum data needed to run the service and never sell your personal information.

1. Who We Are

Puls ("the Platform", "we", "us", "our") is a prediction-market prototype operated by RDM and built on Arc Testnet (chain ID 5042002). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account data: when you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and a basic profile identifier to create your account.
  • Wallet data: a blockchain wallet address is generated for you via Circle's MPC technology. On-chain activity tied to that address is public by nature of the blockchain.
  • Usage data: trades, positions, comments, support tickets, referrals, and similar in-app activity needed to operate Platform features.
  • Technical data: device type, browser, and basic logs used for security, debugging, and abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and wallet.
  • To provide core features — trading, copy-trading, analysis, comments, support, referrals, and notifications.
  • To prevent fraud, multi-accounting, and abuse of the Platform.
  • To improve the product and fix bugs.

4. Wallets & Key Custody

Your wallet is created using Circle MPC (Multi-Party Computation) technology. There is no seed phrase for you to manage. We do not have unilateral access to move your funds outside of the actions you initiate in the app. All tokens are testnet USDC with no real monetary value.

5. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted providers to operate Puls, including:

  • Google — sign-in / authentication
  • Circle — MPC wallets and USDC infrastructure
  • Supabase — authentication and database
  • Polymarket Gamma API — market data
  • UMA — optimistic oracle market resolution
  • Vercel — web hosting

Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers above as needed to run the Platform, or where required by law. On-chain transactions are inherently public.

7. Data Retention

We keep account and usage data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Because this is a testnet prototype, data may be reset or removed as the product evolves.

8. Your Choices

  • You can request deletion of your account data via the in-app support channel.
  • You can stop using the Platform at any time.
  • On-chain records cannot be deleted, as they live on a public blockchain.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is perfectly secure. As this is an experimental testnet product, do not store sensitive personal information on the Platform.

10. Children

Puls is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the in-app support channel or the email provided on our website.
