About Puls

The market for what happens next — a mobile-first prediction market built on Arc, Circle's stablecoin-native L1 where USDC is the gas token.

⚡ Built on Arc 🔐 Circle MPC Wallets 💵 USDC-powered 🔮 UMA Oracle 📊 Polymarket data 🔒 Secured by Vercel

What is Puls?

Puls lets anyone trade on real-world predictions in seconds. You sign in with Google, a gasless Circle MPC smart wallet is created for you instantly — no seed phrase, no ETH — and you swipe to trade markets funded in USDC on Arc Testnet. Markets settle through UMA's optimistic oracle.

How it works

  • Sign in with Google → a Circle MPC smart wallet is created automatically. You control it; there is no seed phrase to lose.
  • Swipe to trade live prediction markets — right for YES, left for NO — funded in USDC, with sub-second finality on Arc.
  • Markets resolve via UMA's optimistic oracle, and winnings are claimable straight to your wallet.
  • Forecasters are creators: they earn USDC per read on premium analysis and a micro-fee when others copy their trades — real per-event nanopayments via x402 on Arc.

Why Arc?

Arc is the only chain where USDC is the native gas token. Users never need ETH, fees are predictable, and trades confirm in under a second — unlocking a UX that isn't possible elsewhere.
